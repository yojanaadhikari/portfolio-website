How I think

I don’t start with screens.

01

Understand what actually needs solving.

I look at the user problem, the business goal and the friction between them before deciding how the interface should work.

02

Make complexity feel simple.

Clear hierarchy, thoughtful interaction and stronger flows help complex products feel understandable, focused and natural without losing the depth they actually need.

03

Design beyond the perfect screen.

Responsive behaviour, edge cases, states, components and development realities are considered from the beginning of the design process.
