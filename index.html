How I think

I don’t start with screens.

01

Understand what actually needs solving.

I look at the user problem, the business goal and the friction between them before deciding what the interface should become.

02

Make complexity feel simple.

Clear hierarchy, thoughtful interaction and better flows should make complex products feel natural.

03

Design beyond the perfect screen.

Responsive behaviour, edge cases, states, components and development realities are part of the design from the beginning.

Behind the work

Pretty screens are only the final 10%.

Before the polished interface, there are flows, wireframes, iterations, components, responsive decisions and plenty of ideas that never make it into the final product.
